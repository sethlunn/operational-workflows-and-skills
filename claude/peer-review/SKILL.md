# Peer Review

Read [../../workflows/peer-review.md](../../workflows/peer-review.md) before starting.

Follow the shared workflow:

- resolve the exact roster and per-peer identifiers first, then gather objective evidence per peer before writing anything
- pull delivered work from Jira and code-review signals from GitHub, and treat both as evidence, not as finished sentences
- ask the user for the inputs only they hold (how the peer used AI, and the user's own observations) and never fabricate them
- shape every claim with the rubric: 2-3 value-anchored strengths and 1-2 growth opportunities, each backed by a real, specific example and its impact
- draft only; write to the private path outside the repo, keep an evidence appendix for verification, and never submit to Lattice
